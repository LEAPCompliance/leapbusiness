Audit & Inspection

Stay Compliant. Stay Inspection Ready.

An audit is a detailed internal review of your own statutory records and practices. An inspection is a physical verification by a government officer, and it can be scheduled, a surprise visit, or triggered by an employee complaint. We prepare you for both.

Pre-Audit Review
A complete internal review across all applicable acts to surface compliance gaps and risks before an officer does. Every area is rated High, Medium or Low risk with a clear remediation plan.
Statutory Documentation
Registers, forms, returns, challans and licence validity checked and brought up to date, so the paperwork holds up when it is actually examined.
On-Site Inspection Support
We attend the inspection with you, liaise with the visiting officer, produce the records requested, and handle questions so your team is not left improvising.
Post-Inspection & Notice Closure
Where observations or notices are raised, we draft the reply, file backdated forms and arrears, and follow the matter through to closure.
Display, Abstracts & Notices
Mandatory notice boards, abstracts and statutory displays verified at every location, one of the first things checked on a site visit and one of the most commonly missed.
Ongoing Advisory
Not a one time exercise. As rules change under the Labour Codes, we keep your records aligned so you stay ready at short notice, not just on audit day.
