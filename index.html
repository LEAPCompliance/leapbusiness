Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a separate consultant for each state?

No. LEAP handles pan-India compliance from a single point of contact. We manage state-specific PT slabs, LWF applicability and Shops and Establishments Acts, so you do not need multiple vendors.

What happens in the free compliance review?

We assess your current status across PF, ESIC, PT, LWF and other applicable acts, identify the key gaps and confirm scope. It takes about 30 minutes and you get a gap report at no cost.

How long does onboarding take?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks. In that time we collect your employee master, registrations and current-month salary data. We then run one full month side by side with your existing process, so nothing changes until the numbers match.

What is a labour law audit and why would we need one?

It is a systematic review of your employment practices, filings, registers and policies against the applicable statutes. It identifies compliance gaps before regulators do.
