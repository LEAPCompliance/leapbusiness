EPFO Wage Ceiling Now ₹25,000

Officially notified and effective 17 September 2026. Here's what changed and what to do about it, right now.

Updated 17 September 2026 · PF / EPFO
Now Official

The EPFO wage ceiling hike is no longer just a Cabinet approval, it's law. The Ministry of Labour & Employment has issued Gazette Notification S.O. 5109(E), dated 17 September 2026, raising the EPF statutory wage ceiling from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month under Chapter III of the Code on Social Security, 2020, effective the same day. It supersedes the earlier notification S.O. 2702(E) dated 29 May 2026.

The shift

Employees earning between ₹15,000 and ₹25,000 a month, previously outside mandatory coverage, now fall within it under EPF, EPS, and EDLI, effective 17 September 2026.

What it costs, per employee at the ceiling wage

Wage Ceiling Employee Pays Employer Pays
₹15,000 (earlier) ₹1,800 ₹1,950
Pension ₹1,250 + PF ₹550 + Admin ₹75 + EDLI ₹75
₹25,000 (now) ₹3,000 ₹3,250
Pension ₹2,083 + PF ₹917 + Admin ₹125 + EDLI ₹125

Net effect once implemented: roughly ₹1,200 more deducted from an affected employee's pay, and about ₹1,300 more in employer cost, per employee at the new ceiling.

This is live from today. Payroll and HRMS configuration should be updated without further delay for every employee now falling in the ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 band. Contact LEAP if you'd like help getting this right.

What to do now

  • Identify all employees earning ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month
  • Bring them under EPF, EPS & EDLI coverage
  • Update payroll/HRMS wage ceiling settings to ₹25,000
  • Recompute employer cost and flag it to your finance team

Additional benefits notified alongside the hike

  • 8.25% interest on EPF balance for the current financial year
  • Employees can withdraw up to 75% of their contribution online, as per need
  • Claims up to ₹5,00,000 auto-settled within 3 days under CITES

Relief for employers

Part of the added cost can be recovered through the PMVBRY Scheme, an incentive of up to ₹3,000 per employee per month, by submitting Aadhaar and KYC details. Available for 2 years (non-manufacturing) or 4 years (manufacturing).

Sources: Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II Section 3(ii), No. 4918, S.O. 5109(E), Ministry of Labour & Employment, 17 September 2026; EPFO Regional Office Thane South (Ghatkopar) Press Release, 17 September 2026; PIB Delhi, Release ID 2310812, 16 September 2026.
