EPFO Wage Ceiling Raised to ₹25,000

Cabinet approved the hike on 16 September 2026. Here's what changed and what to do about it.

16 September 2026 · PF / EPFO

The Union Cabinet has approved raising the EPFO wage ceiling for mandatory coverage from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month. Over 51 lakh additional employees across India are expected to come under mandatory PF, Pension, and EDLI coverage once the change is formally notified.

The shift

Employees earning between ₹15,000 and ₹25,000 a month, currently outside mandatory coverage, will fall within it once the ceiling change takes effect.

What it costs, per employee at the ceiling wage

Wage Ceiling Employee Pays Employer Pays
₹15,000 (current) ₹1,800 ₹1,950
Pension ₹1,250 + PF ₹550 + Admin ₹75 + EDLI ₹75
₹25,000 (proposed) ₹3,000 ₹3,250
Pension ₹2,083 + PF ₹917 + Admin ₹125 + EDLI ₹125

Net effect once implemented: roughly ₹1,200 more deducted from an affected employee's pay, and about ₹1,300 more in employer cost, per employee at the new ceiling.

Before you touch your HRMS: this is a Cabinet approval, not a live rule yet. EPFO and the Ministry of Labour still need to issue the formal notification and scheme amendments. Please don't change payroll configuration until that notification is out. Contact LEAP and we'll flag it the moment it's issued.

What you can start doing now

  • Pull the list of employees drawing ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month
  • Check who is currently excluded from PF and why
  • Estimate the added monthly cost to your payroll
  • Flag this to your finance team so it gets budgeted for

Source: Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India (Release ID: 2310812), 16 September 2026.
