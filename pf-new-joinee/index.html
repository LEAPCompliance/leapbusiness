New joinee PF enrolment: one link to share

Send this page to every new joinee. It shows what to give you, how to get a UAN, and how to answer Form 11 correctly.

Step 3

Form 11: the pension question

Item 7 (earlier PF member) and item 8 (earlier pension member) must each be answered Yes or No. Ask the joinee to check the PF passbook rather than guess.

  1. Open the PF passbook

    Log in with the UAN on the Member passbook page, or use the passbook in UMANG.

  2. Open the earlier account

    Pick the PF account of the previous establishment.

  3. Look at the Pension account

    See whether contributions are available in the Pension account.

HR tips

  • Send it early

    Share the link with the offer letter and ask for the UAN before the joining date.

  • Keep Form 11

    Signed, complete and on file for future reference.

  • Register your DSC

    Without it, a previous PF transfer cannot be approved by you and the member has to file Form 13 on paper.
