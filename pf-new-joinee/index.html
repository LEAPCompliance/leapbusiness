For new joinees

Your PF enrolment: what to keep ready and how to get your UAN

Your employer is enrolling you in the Provident Fund. This page lists what you need to hand over and shows how to generate your UAN yourself on the UMANG app, using face authentication.

Start here

Do you already have a UAN?

UAN is the 12-digit Universal Account Number that identifies you with EPFO. Everything else on this page depends on this one answer.

I had PF at an earlier job

Use the same UAN. Do not create a new one.

Your UAN stays the same when you change jobs. Give it to your new employer along with your previous PF member ID and the date you left your last job.

If you never activated it, you can activate it on the UMANG app with the same face authentication shown below.

Cannot find it? Use Know Your UAN on the EPFO Member portal, or check old payslips and PF messages on your phone for a 12-digit number. If you still cannot trace it, tell your HR and we will help.

This is my first PF job

Generate your UAN yourself on the UMANG app.

Since 1 August 2025, a new UAN is generated only by the member, through Aadhaar face authentication in the UMANG app. Your employer cannot generate it for you, except in a few cases such as international workers.

Follow the steps below, then send your UAN to HR.

First-time members

Generate your UAN in the UMANG app

Keep your phone, your Aadhaar number and the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar with you. The OTP goes to that mobile number.

  1. Install the UMANG appIt is the Government of India's official app.
    Google Play App Store
  2. Install the Aadhaar FaceRD appThis is the app that does the face scan for UMANG.
    Google Play
    On an iPhone, follow the instructions shown inside UMANG, or ask your HR to check with us.
  3. Open UAN Allotment and ActivationIn UMANG, open EPFO services. Under UAN services through face authentication, choose UAN Allotment and Activation.
  4. Enter your Aadhaar and mobile numberAccept the consent, then enter the OTP that arrives on your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  5. Do the face scanHold the phone at eye level in good light with your face fully visible. Your face is matched with the photo in the Aadhaar database.
  6. Note your UAN and download the e-UAN cardYour UAN is sent to you by SMS. You can download the e-UAN card from the UMANG app or from the EPFO Member portal. The UAN is activated on the Member portal as part of this process.
  7. Send your UAN and e-UAN card to HRA PDF of the e-UAN card along with the UAN is what your employer needs to onboard you with EPFO.
Check what the app shows. Your name, date of birth and father's name are taken from your Aadhaar. If anything is wrong there, get your Aadhaar corrected first, because your PF record follows it.

Hand these to HR

Details your employer needs from you

These are the details asked for in EPFO's Form 11, plus your UAN. Give them exactly as they appear on your Aadhaar. A spelling or date difference is the most common reason an enrolment gets stuck.

Everyone
  • Full name, exactly as on Aadhaar
  • Father's name or spouse's name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender and marital status
  • Mobile number and email ID
  • Aadhaar number
  • PAN, if you have one
  • Bank account number and IFSC code
  • Your UAN, and a PDF of your e-UAN card
Only if you were a PF member before
  • Your earlier UAN
  • Previous PF account (member ID) number
  • Date you left your previous job
  • Scheme certificate number, if one was issued
  • Pension Payment Order (PPO) number, if one was issued
Only for international workers
  • Country of origin, passport number and passport validity
Form 11 also asks for self-attested copies of your KYC documents. Keep a clear copy of your Aadhaar, your PAN and your bank passbook or cancelled cheque ready.
Mandatory declaration

Form 11: what it is and why the pension question matters

Form 11 is the declaration every person taking up employment in an establishment covered by the EPF Scheme or the Employees' Pension Scheme signs. Your employer keeps it for future reference. It records who you are, your KYC details, any earlier PF or pension membership, and an undertaking that lets EPFO use your Aadhaar to verify you.

Item 7: earlier PF member?

Were you ever a member of the Employees' Provident Fund Scheme at any earlier job, even for a short time? If yes, your earlier UAN and PF number go in item 9.

Item 8: earlier pension member?

Were you ever a member of the Employees' Pension Scheme? Answer this one carefully. It records your earlier pension membership, which affects how your pension account is set up with your new employer.

If you are not sure, say so to HR before you sign. Your previous employer's HR, or your earlier PF passbook, can confirm whether pension contributions were made. Answering Yes or No by guess is what causes problems later.

What happens next

After you share your details

1. Linked to your employer

Your employer, with LEAP's help, links your UAN to the establishment and records your date of joining.

2. KYC updated

Aadhaar and PAN are updated from the employer side. Your bank details can only be added by you on the Member portal, so do that early to avoid a stuck claim later.

3. Contributions show up

Once the monthly return is filed, the contributions appear in your PF passbook on the Member portal.
