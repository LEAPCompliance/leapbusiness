Method and assumptions

  • Employer’s 12% is split as EPS 8.33% (on wages capped at ₹15,000 for domestic workers) and EPF 3.67% (balance). Where the employer voluntarily contributes on PF wages above the ceiling, the excess flows entirely into EPF.
  • International workers’ EPS contribution is computed on full gross wages without the ceiling, per the EPFO circular dated 01.10.2008.
  • EDLI is always capped at ₹15,000. EPF admin charges are 0.5% with a monthly minimum of ₹500 per establishment (₹75 for non-functional establishments).

ESIC Wage Base & Contribution (Code on Wages 50% Rule)

Enter your salary structure component by component. The calculator applies the Code on Wages 50% rule, adds back any excess exclusions, derives the ESIC wage base, checks eligibility against the wage ceiling and computes both contributions.

Result

Wage base after the 50% rule, and monthly ESIC liability.

Method and assumptions

  • The 50% rule: where components marked “Excluded (counts toward 50%)” exceed 50% of total remuneration, the excess is added back into the wage base. This mirrors the proviso to the definition of wages under the Code on Wages, 2019, which the Social Security Code adopts for ESIC.
  • “Excluded (NOT in 50% test)” takes an item out of wages without letting it affect the 50% test, typically used for gratuity and retrenchment compensation.
  • Eligibility is a simple monthly check of the ESIC wage base against the ceiling. Actual coverage also depends on contribution periods: an employee who crosses the ceiling mid-period continues to contribute until the end of that contribution period.
  • Overtime is included when computing the contribution payable, but is disregarded when testing whether an employee is within the wage ceiling. Treat the overtime row accordingly for each purpose.

Gratuity Calculator

Compute gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and the Code on Social Security, 2020. Supports permanent and fixed-term employees. Results are indicative; verify against the wage definition applicable to your establishment.

Indicative computation. Subject to your establishment’s wage definition.

Method and assumptions

  • Monthly-rated employees: gratuity equals (last drawn wages × 15 × years of service) ÷ 26.
  • Service rounding: a fraction of more than six months in the final year is treated as one full year. Fixed-term employees under the Code on Social Security, 2020 receive gratuity on a pro-rata basis once one year of service is completed.
  • Eligibility is tested on actual continuous service, not on the rounded figure. Several High Courts have held that 4 years and 240 days in the fifth year qualifies; that reading is not applied here by default.
  • The five-year qualifying period does not apply where service ends due to death or disablement.
  • Statutory ceiling of ₹20,00,000 applies under Section 4(3) of the 1972 Act and Section 53(2) of the Code on Social Security, 2020.
  • Wages must include basic, dearness allowance and retaining allowance. The Code’s 50% floor rule under Section 2(88) may apply once notified.

Statutory Bonus Calculator

Compute the annual statutory bonus payable under the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965. Applies the ₹21,000 eligibility ceiling, the ₹7,000 or scheduled-minimum-wage calculation floor, the 8.33% to 20% band and pro-rating by days worked.

8.33% to 20%

Rate set by allocable surplus under Sections 10 and 11. Default 8.33% is the statutory minimum.

Result

Annual statutory bonus payable.

Method and assumptions

  • Eligibility ceiling of ₹21,000/month was last revised by the Payment of Bonus (Amendment) Act, 2015 with retrospective effect from 01.04.2014.
  • Where the employee’s salary exceeds ₹7,000 and the scheduled minimum wage for that scheduled employment is higher than ₹7,000, bonus is computed on the minimum wage; otherwise on ₹7,000.
  • Bonus must be paid within 8 months from the close of the accounting year (Section 19). Disqualifications under Section 9 (fraud, riotous behaviour or theft) are not modelled here.
