Your ESIC registration: what to keep ready and how to validate your Aadhaar

Your employer will register you as an Insured Person. Give every detail in one go, then validate your Aadhaar within 30 days so your benefits continue without a break.

Read this first

Three things every new joinee should know

ESIC has introduced a new registration module in its ERP system, with Aadhaar at the centre. If the details are right at the start, your registration is done once and stays clean.

Registration is through Aadhaar

A new Insured Person (IP) can now be registered only through Aadhaar. Your name and other details must match what UIDAI holds.

Your mobile gets an OTP

Your employer validates your mobile number during registration, and later the OTP in the ESIC Health Connect app goes to that number. Give the number you actually use.

Validate within 30 days

Aadhaar validation must be completed within 30 days of registration so that all benefits continue without a break.

Why we push this hard. In our experience, Insured Persons whose Aadhaar is not validated in time get deactivated, and getting them back means extra requests and delay.

Give every detail in one go

An IP number can be generated with only the basic fields. We still ask for the complete form at the time of registration, because correcting or adding details later is slow: each change goes through a request that needs ESIC's approval, and in our experience it takes a month or more. A complete first entry avoids all of that.

About you
  • Full name, exactly as on Aadhaar
  • Gender and date of birth
  • Father's or husband's name
  • Marital status
  • Aadhaar number
  • PAN, if you have one
  • Previous ESIC number, if you were ever registered before
Contact
  • Mobile number, preferably the one linked to AadhaarThe OTP will be sent here.
  • Email ID
Address and bank
  • Present address, complete
  • Permanent address, or confirm it is the same
  • Bank account number and IFSC code
Nominee
  • Nominee's name and relationship with you
  • Nominee's address and mobile number
Family members
  • Spouse, children and dependent parentsName, date of birth, relationship, and whether they live with you. If not, the town and state.
Documents to keep ready
  • Aadhaar copy
  • PAN copy, if you have one
  • Passport-size photoColour photo on a white background, at least 200 by 200 pixels.
  • Bank passbook first page or a cancelled chequeUsed to verify the account number and IFSC.
Within 30 days of registration

Validate your Aadhaar on the ESIC Health Connect app

Once your employer has registered you and given you your Insurance Number, you can validate your Aadhaar yourself with a face scan. It takes only your phone.

  1. Install ESIC Health ConnectThe official app of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation.
    Google Play App Store
  2. Choose Verify Insurance NumberOpen the app and select this option to validate your Aadhaar.
  3. Enter your Insurance Number and tap Get OTPYour employer or LEAP will give you this number once registration is done.
  4. Enter the OTPIt arrives on the mobile number registered with ESIC.
  5. Choose Face Authentication and complete the scanFace the camera in good light.
  6. Confirm and proceedWhen the app shows that your details matched, accept the declaration and proceed. Your Aadhaar status then shows as Verified on your e-Pehchan card.
If the app says your details do not match. The name, date of birth or other details held by ESIC differ from your Aadhaar. A request goes through your employer and the ESIC branch for approval, and a reference number is generated. Where a detail is wrong, it has to be corrected, so tell HR at once.
You can also be validated by your employer or by an ESIC Branch Manager using a biometric device, either at registration or later. Ask HR which is easier for you.
