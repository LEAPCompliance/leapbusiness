Method and assumptions

  • An establishment with 50 or more employees must provide a creche facility within the prescribed distance (Section 11A).
  • Work-from-home may be permitted after the leave period on terms mutually agreed (Section 5(5)).
  • Dismissal during pregnancy or maternity leave is prohibited (Section 12). Discharge during this period still entitles the woman to maternity benefit and medical bonus.
  • Nursing breaks under Section 11 are payable till the child attains 15 months.
  • Average daily wage is taken as the monthly wage divided by 30. Related: Code on Social Security, CTC calculator.
