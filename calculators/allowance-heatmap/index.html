How to read this reckoner

Included heads count towards wages for the proviso (basic + DA + retaining allowance ≥ 50% of total remuneration). Excluded heads are listed in Section 2(y)(a) to (k). Conditional heads depend on how the component is structured in the salary letter and on documentation.
