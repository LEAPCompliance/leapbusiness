Central law

Provident Fund (PF)

From registration to final settlement, every rupee reconciled.

What it is

Provident Fund is the retirement and social security contribution shared by employer and employee. It covers the member's PF account, the pension component and the insurance component. The 2026 Scheme also recognises voluntary contributions for higher earners and adds a clearer framework for contractors, principal employers and international workers.

Who needs it

Establishments that cross the coverage threshold under the Code, and those that opt in voluntarily. If you are unsure whether you or your contractors are covered, that is a ten minute conversation, not a guess.

What we handle

  • Establishment registration on the EPFO portal
  • DSC and e-Sign activation for the authorised signatory
  • Guidance on government employment and EPFO schemes, with registration and enrolment support wherever you qualify
  • Employee UAN onboarding: guiding each employee through self-generation on the UMANG app with face authentication, then linking them to your establishment, with Aadhaar and PAN updates from the employer side
  • Verification of PF calculation, with clear guidance on what goes into PF wages and why
  • Challan filing with precision, every month
  • Your day to day PF queries, answered as they come up
  • Member support on withdrawals, transfers and advance withdrawal queries
  • Death case settlement support for PF, EPS and EDLI
  • Yearly reconciliation statement and employee Form 3A, maintained and ready
  • Date of leaving updates on the portal
  • Contractor and principal employer reporting under the 2026 Scheme
Legal basis

Code on Social Security, 2020 and the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026, which replaced the EPF Act, 1952 and the EPF Scheme, 1952.

ESIC (Employees' State Insurance)

Medical and cash cover for your people, filed right the first time.

What it is

ESIC is a contributory insurance scheme giving employees and their dependants medical care, sickness benefit, maternity benefit, disablement benefit and dependants' benefit. The employer collects and pays both the employer and employee shares.

Who needs it

Establishments that cross the coverage threshold under the Code, and establishments in notified hazardous occupations. Individual employees are covered based on their wages under the Code's wage definition. We run the coverage test for every employee, every month.

What we handle

  • Employer registration and insured person registration from the first day of employment
  • Monthly coverage test, including employees who cross the wage limit mid period
  • Contribution computation with correct rounding
  • Monthly contribution filing and challan payment
  • Contribution period tracking so benefits are not lost by a wrong entry
  • Exit and transfer entries, and insured person data corrections
  • Benefit claims support for employees and dependants
  • Inspection readiness and response to ESIC notices and queries
Legal basis

Employees' State Insurance framework under the Code on Social Security, 2020. The Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 was repealed on 21 November 2025, and its rules, regulations and schemes continue under the Code's transitional provisions.

Where employers slip

  • Coverage decisions made once at joining and never re-tested as pay changes
  • Late insured person registration, which leaves an injured or hospitalised employee without benefit
  • Small rounding differences that break reconciliation with the portal
  • Wrong contribution period assignment for joiners and leavers
  • Ignoring the transition: the rules are moving from the old Act to the Code, and filing practice needs to follow

What you get

A monthly ESIC pack (computation, challan, payment proof), a coverage exception list, an insured person register health check, and claims assistance when an employee needs it.

State law

Professional Tax (PT)

Right slab, right month, right return.

What it is

Professional Tax is a state levy on income from employment and professions. The employer deducts it from salary and pays it to the state. Professionals and business persons who are not on a payroll pay it on their own account.

Who needs it

Employers in states that levy PT, for employees above the state's exemption limit, and individuals and firms covered by the state schedule. Maharashtra distinguishes between an employer registration that deducts from staff (PTRC) and a registration for tax payable on one's own account (PTEC). Other states have their own structures.

What we handle

  • PTRC and PTEC registration, and amendments when the business changes
  • Salary to slab mapping, including state specific rules such as Maharashtra's separate treatment by gender
  • Monthly or annual challans and returns, matched to your liability profile
  • Multi state PT mapping for employees who work or relocate across state lines
  • Notice handling, assessment orders and appeals
Legal basis

State Professional Tax law. In Maharashtra, the Maharashtra State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1975. A state law, so it sits outside the Code on Social Security.

  • Employees who moved states mid year with the old state's treatment still applied
  • Year end adjustments missed, leaving small annual shortfalls per employee

Slab verified deduction in your payroll, returns and challans filed on time, and a yearly PT reconciliation.

State law

Labour Welfare Fund (LWF)

A small filing that is easy to forget and costly to miss.

What it is

The Labour Welfare Fund pools small contributions from employees and employers to fund welfare activities for workers, such as medical assistance, scholarships, recreation and skill development. Contributions are periodic and rules differ in every state.

Who needs it

Establishments in states that have an LWF law, once they cross that state's applicability threshold. Managerial and certain supervisory staff are often excluded, and the definition varies by state.

What we handle

  • LWF registration on the state portal
  • Employee eligibility mapping: who is in and who is out
  • Deduction and employer share computation each period
  • On time payment and return filing
  • Handling of unpaid accumulations and regulator queries
  • Multi state LWF calendars for employers with staff in several states
Legal basis

State Labour Welfare Fund law. In Maharashtra, the Maharashtra Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1953. A state law, so it sits outside the Code on Social Security.

One wage definition, four different answers

The Code on Social Security, 2020 defines "wages" in a way that can add allowances and other pay elements back into the wage base. That definition drives Provident Fund, ESIC, gratuity and maternity benefit. Professional Tax and LWF follow their own state definitions.
