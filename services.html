The four Labour Codes were notified on 21 November 2025, and the Central Government issued the final Central Rules on 8 May 2026. State rules are at different stages — over 30 states and Union Territories have notified rules for at least one Code as of July 2026, while others (including Maharashtra) are still finalising theirs. We track each Code as it comes into force in your operating states and move the corresponding filings across without disrupting ongoing compliance. See our Labour Codes guide for details.
